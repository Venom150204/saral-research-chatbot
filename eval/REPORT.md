## How each metric is computed

- **Precision@k / Recall@k / F1@k**: relevant passages in the top k ÷ k; ÷ all relevant passages in the deck; their harmonic mean. **nDCG@k** rewards relevant passages ranked higher. **MRR**: 1 ÷ rank of the first relevant passage.
- **Citation precision / recall**: computed from the slides of the bullets an answer cites ([S#]) against the labelled gold slides. No model involved.
- **RAGAS faithfulness**: the judge splits the answer into statements and checks each against the context; score = supported ÷ all statements. **Context precision**: the judge marks each cited bullet useful or not for the reference answer; precision is averaged over ranks. **Context recall**: the judge splits the reference answer into statements and checks each is in the cited bullets.
- **Source-overlap proxy** (assignment): every figure in a cited sentence must appear in its cited bullet (compared in written form), plus embedding cosine similarity between the sentence and the bullet.
- **Citation coverage**: deck-claim units carrying at least one citation ÷ deck-claim units (background explanation and framing are labelled separately and reviewed as such).
- **Claims supported**: SARAL's independent checker (a different model from the writer) per cited claim.
- **Limits**: measured by code (spoken length at 150 words per minute, characters, item counts).
